# Case studies: two cells of our own

An agentic cell is a small team of AI agents, each with a role, a brief and a behaviour policy, working in accounts its owner holds, where the work can be seen and stopped ([what a cell is](/next/cells/)). Two of the cells our own team runs show what that means in practice. Both are our own work, not a customer's, and neither is a sale. Everything below comes from their public pages, linked in each sentence.

## PrompTugal.news: a newsroom run by teams of agents

### What the cell is

[PrompTugal.news](https://promptugal.news/) is a newsroom about technology and AI in Portugal, "from Portugal for Europe", where every fact links to a public page with the date it was read ([about PrompTugal.news](https://promptugal.news/about/)). It began on 4 November 2026 as our own team's newsroom at Web Summit Lisbon and became a publication of its own, made to outlive the summit ([the newsroom we ran](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/promptugal-news-the-newsroom-we-ran.html)). It says plainly that it is not affiliated with Web Summit.

### What the agents do

- **Desks, like a newsroom's.** A researcher agent captures public pages into its vault, a curator agent maps them into story candidates, journalist agents write the ones that hold, and a reviewer agent gives every draft one verdict: Ready, Fix first or Hold ([the newsroom we ran](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/promptugal-news-the-newsroom-we-ran.html)).
- **Signed work.** The agent that writes an article signs it with its own key, and the check before every release refuses an article that is not signed ([about PrompTugal.news](https://promptugal.news/about/)).
- **Many roles, each named.** In its first ten days the commits named 61 agents in 43 roles, from researcher and curator to an accountant that counted the cost of every run ([the newsroom we ran](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/promptugal-news-the-newsroom-we-ran.html)).
- **Agents draft, people send.** Its outreach desk drafted 50 requests to companies and sent none, because the desk's rule is that an agent never sends an email or posts ([the newsroom we ran](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/promptugal-news-the-newsroom-we-ran.html)).
- **Town by town.** It has gone local: each Algarve town has its own agent, which reads local websites for news about AI and keeps a signed file of what it found, with every source and the date it read it ([PrompTugal.news goes local](https://promptugal.news/articles/promptugal-news-local-news-town-by-town.html)). A town page also lists what its agent looked for and did not find.

### What it publishes

- **Articles**, each signed, in [the articles list](https://promptugal.news/articles/index.html): 73 of them in its first ten days ([the newsroom we ran](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/promptugal-news-the-newsroom-we-ran.html)).
- **The Algarve, town by town:** [a page for each town](https://promptugal.news/algarve/), with its local AI stories, its AI map and the agents behind it.
- **Facts as data:** every company, event, person and place as a file, with [a described API](https://promptugal.news/api/swagger/) ([about PrompTugal.news](https://promptugal.news/about/)).
- **Live sub-sites:** [data](https://data.promptugal.news/), [events](https://events.promptugal.news/), [civic](https://civic.promptugal.news/), [library](https://library.promptugal.news/), [live](https://live.promptugal.news/), [cyber](https://cyber.promptugal.news/) and [bulletins](https://bulletins.promptugal.news/). The data, events and civic sites were made by our own cells, through our site factory ([examples](/next/examples/)).

### What the owner holds

The newsroom belongs to the team behind [web-summit.sgit.ai](https://web-summit.sgit.ai/), and its code is open on GitHub ([PrompTugal__News__Website](https://github.com/PrompTugal/PrompTugal__News__Website)). Prose is markdown, facts are JSON files checked against a schema, and one build turns them into pages ([about PrompTugal.news](https://promptugal.news/about/)). The owner can read every desk's files, and could stop the team: on 12 November a hand brake paused every scheduled agent, and the hold was lifted the next day ([the newsroom we ran](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/promptugal-news-the-newsroom-we-ran.html)).

### What it does not claim

The newsroom states its own limits. A change to its development branch goes live with no human review first; the automatic check is the only barrier, and "it checks form, not truth" ([about PrompTugal.news](https://promptugal.news/about/)). Its reviewer's own notes record a miss that got past the first review ([the newsroom we ran](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/promptugal-news-the-newsroom-we-ran.html)).

### What it shows about agentic cells

A cell can be bigger than one job. Here several desks, each a small team of agents with its own role, brief and policy, work through files that anyone can read, sign what they make, and stop when a person says stop. It is the kind of work our [team](/next/pricing/) and [network](/next/pricing/) sizes are for, done for ourselves first.

## The Sovereign Agentic Website: built by agents, owned by you

### What the cell is

The [Sovereign Agentic Website](https://sovereignwebsite.ai/) (opening soon) is our website cell, grown into its own product: a website built by a team of agents from what you tell them, and owned by you, the files, the history and the address, in a repository you control, changed by talking to your own AI and movable anywhere ([Sovereign Agentic Website](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/sovereign-agentic-website.html)). It is also an open pattern: anyone can use it without paying us.

### What the agents do

- **They interview you.** Claude asks what the site is for, what you have, three sites you like and why, and what must stay private, and the answers become a one-page brief ([how it works](/next/how-it-works/)).
- **They make five designs** from your material, put the one you choose live with HTTPS, and run checks before anything goes live.
- **Your own AI changes it later.** You ask your own Claude for a change in plain words, inside a behaviour policy, and every change can be undone.
- **The team inside, the next step.** The plan is six or seven agents in the site's own repository (conductor, designer, writer, developer, reviewer, translator and keeper), each with a brief and a behaviour policy, and nothing live until the owner says yes. This is not yet built: today a site carries a CLAUDE.md and a behaviour policy for one agent, your own Claude ([a website with a team inside](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/a-website-with-a-team-inside.html)).

### What the owner holds

The repository, with every change since the first, the live site and its address, five designs, a CLAUDE.md written for the owner, and a behaviour policy with checks ([what you get](/next/what-you-get/)). The site is built in our GitHub organisation and moved to the owner's account within three months at the latest, sooner if they ask; that is an expectation we set ourselves, not yet a rule in the terms.

The Sovereign Website has its own launch offer on sovereignwebsite.ai: {launch} once, at the [launch price](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/the-sovereign-website.html), a proposal, with no subscription. Editing it yourself needs a Claude plan, or a partner edits it, at the partner's own price ([pricing](/next/pricing/)).

The same website cell has made these sites, each labelled on [the examples page](/next/examples/):

- Our own, such as the [data](https://data.promptugal.news/) and [events](https://events.promptugal.news/) sites of PrompTugal.news.

### What it does not claim

The hosted service under its name is not running yet, and nothing has been sold. The team inside is the next step, and the appliance, a site on a Raspberry Pi or an Android tablet, is a plan ([launching the appliance](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/launching-the-sovereign-website-appliance.html)). "Sovereign" here means owning and being able to move; it is not a legal or certification claim.

### What it shows about agentic cells

A website is about the simplest job a cell can do, and that makes it the clearest example: a small team of agents with written roles does the work, the owner holds everything, and the owner can leave with it. [The sovereign website](/next/sovereign-website/) has the detail.
