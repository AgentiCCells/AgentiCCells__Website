# You can leave, with your site, whenever you want

A website you own, control and can take anywhere. Of everything we offer, this is the difference that matters most, and we put it first rather than in the small print.

{{figure:can-you-leave}}

## Why it matters most

Everyone has AI now. The designs and the price can be matched. What a platform cannot match without changing its business is this: the customer can leave, with their site, whenever they want, and owes nobody anything. Dinis Cruz, in [no lock-in](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/no-lock-in.html): "None of this is hidden; it is the business model. A platform earns every month because you stay."

## What we do instead

- **Your repository.** The site is files in your own GitHub repository, with every change since the first.
- **Any host.** Static files run on GitHub Pages for free, or on any web host. [Put your site online](https://agenticcells.ai/deploy/) links to each host's own instructions.
- **Your Claude.** You change it by talking to your own Claude, not to our platform. We have no platform.
- **Paid once.** No subscription from us, so nothing to stop paying for.
- **Yours within three months.** We expect every site we build to move into its owner's ownership within three months: the repository transferred to your GitHub account and the address pointed where you choose. We hold it only to finish the work. It is an expectation today, not yet a rule we enforce.
- **Open.** The code and the policies are open source, so even we cannot lock anyone in.

## Leaving us

Leaving us is the same as leaving anyone else, except there is nothing to ask for. After hand-over you hold the repository, so you can move it to another host, give it to a partner to look after, or keep changing it yourself. If you leave before hand-over, ask, and we move the repository to you.
