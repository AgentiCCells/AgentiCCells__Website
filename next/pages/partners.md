# Partners: you bring the business, we build the site

This page is about the smallest cell, the sovereign website. A cell is a small team of AI agents, each with a role, a brief and written rules, working in your own accounts ([what a cell is](/next/cells/)).

You know people who want a website. You sell it to them; we build it behind you and hand it over in a GitHub repository your customer owns. Then you sell the care around it, at your own prices.

## The deal

- **You sell** the site to your customer and keep the relationship. You find them, agree what they want, and collect their material.
- **We build** the site: the interview, five designs, the live site with HTTPS, the instructions for Claude and the checks, and the move of the repository to your customer's GitHub account.
- **The sale is split 50/50** when we are involved, as the published [partner terms](https://agenticcells.ai/partners/#partner-terms) say. Whoever has the customer invoices them and pays the other side its half. Larger projects are agreed case by case.
- **Everything after is yours:** hosting, support, changes and monthly care, at prices you set. We take no share of it and sell no subscriptions.

## A worked example (simulation)

A simulation, not a sale: you sell one site to a customer at our £50 launch price, and under the [partner terms](https://agenticcells.ai/partners/#partner-terms) the sale is split 50/50, so £25 is yours and £25 is ours at the launch price. You invoice the customer, because you have them, and pay us our half. What you then charge for care and changes is yours alone.

At the launch price a partner's share of the build is small, so most of a partner's income is meant to come from the care around the site. How a sale above the launch price is split is not yet written down: agree it with us before the sale.

## A deal, from first call to after care

1. **Find.** You find the customer and agree what they want, and the price.
2. **Material.** Their photos, videos and words come in, starting with the interview.
3. **Set up.** We set up the repository, the address and the site.
4. **Designs.** The agents make five designs; the customer chooses, with you.
5. **Hand-over.** The repository moves to the customer's GitHub, within three months at the latest (an expectation we set ourselves, not yet a rule in the terms). You get admin access if the customer agrees.
6. **After.** You host, support, change and charge for it as you choose.

## The sovereignty line

The customer owns the repository; the partner keeps the relationship. Selling a site your customer can take anywhere is not a weakness for a partner: it is the reason to buy from you rather than rent from a platform, and it is why they come back to you for the care around it.

## Three kinds of partner

From Dinis Cruz's [partners sell, we build](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/partners-sell-we-build.html):

- **The freelancer.** One person who talks to people and can say: for this amount, I will give you a website, an agentic email and a workflow. [The freelancer's page](https://agenticcells.ai/partners/freelancer/).
- **The small consultancy.** A firm with customers who want this: a site for each client, the same team every time. [The consultancy's page](https://agenticcells.ai/partners/consultancy/).
- **The larger firm.** Bigger projects, with our help and a network of experts involved, case by case. [The firm's page](https://agenticcells.ai/partners/firm/).

## The rest of the terms, in short

The partner terms are kept on the live [partners page](https://agenticcells.ai/partners/#partner-terms); if this page and that one differ, that one holds.

- **The customer owns** the site, the repository and the material. **You own** the relationship.
- **Payment terms** are agreed per deal.
- **Our code and policies are open.** You may keep your own repositories and your customers' work private.
- **Nobody is named** publicly, customer or partner, without their agreement.
- **Any country, any language.** You invoice in your own currency, under your own law.

## Who builds it

Even the smallest site is made by seven roles, each played by an AI agent with a written job description and rules it works under, each signing its work: a lead, a designer, a developer, a writer, a researcher, a reviewer and a security reviewer. [How it works](/next/how-it-works/) says what each one does.

Everything we use is open source. A partner who takes it and builds sites without us is still running the same technology under the same kind of rules, and we will say so.
