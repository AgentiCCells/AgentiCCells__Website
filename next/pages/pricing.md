## Running it costs almost nothing

GitHub Pages serves a site from a public repository on GitHub's free plan, at a github.io address or on your own domain, with HTTPS ([GitHub's documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages), read on 19 November 2026). Your own domain, if you want one, is paid to the registrar you choose. Editing is done by your own Claude.

## Two honest notes

- **Editing needs a Claude plan, or a partner.** You change the site by talking to your own Claude, so to edit it yourself you need a Claude plan. If you would rather not, a partner can edit it for you. The platforms bundle their editor; we do not.
- **Partners set their own build price.** A partner who sells you a site sets their own price for the build and for the care around it. The launch price is what we charge.
