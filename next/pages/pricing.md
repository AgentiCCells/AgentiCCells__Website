## The launch price

The launch price is £50, paid once ([the sovereign website](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/the-sovereign-website.html)). The usual price is £500, and the earlier launch price was £250 ([you bring the business, we build the site](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/you-bring-the-business.html)); the launch price now is lower than both.

The launch price may change, and when it does, this page and the price block above change together. Nothing has been sold yet.

What it buys is on [what you get](/next/what-you-get/): the repository in your name, the live site with HTTPS, five designs, a CLAUDE.md for you, a behaviour policy and checks, and ownership within three months (an expectation we set ourselves, not yet a rule in the terms).

## Running it can cost nothing

The site is plain files, so you can run it anywhere. GitHub Pages hosts it free when the repository is public and the site is not an online shop or a site for business transactions ([GitHub's terms](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/github-pages-limits), read 19 November 2026). A private repository needs a paid GitHub plan, and a shop or a site that takes payments needs another host ([the same page](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/github-pages-limits)). Any static host will do, and many have a free tier, such as [Cloudflare Pages](https://developers.cloudflare.com/pages/platform/limits/) (its Free plan, read 19 November 2026). Your own domain, if you want one, is paid to the registrar you choose. Editing is done by your own Claude, which needs a Claude plan, or by a partner.
