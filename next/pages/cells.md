# Agentic cells

An agentic cell is a small team of AI agents doing one focused job for you. Each agent has a role, a brief that says what it does, and a behaviour policy that says what it may and may not touch. The agents work together, in your own accounts, and you can see what they do and stop them. A cell is the unit of what we sell ([the cell is the unit](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/the-cell-is-the-unit.html)).

{{figure:cell}}

## What every cell has

- **An objective:** the one job it exists for, said in your words.
- **A deliverable:** what you have at the end, such as a live site, drafted replies or a debrief.
- **A behaviour policy for every agent:** what it may reach, what it was asked to do, and what it will never touch ([feature behaviour policies](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/feature-behaviour-policies.html)).
- **The parts the job needs:** a Claude session, a repository, a website, a schedule, a connector, and a person who owns it.

## How a cell runs

- **In one session.** The smallest cells run in one session: a conductor and its agents, side by side. Bigger cells span several sessions and several people.
- **With briefs and policies.** Each agent works from its brief, under its behaviour policy, and the work is kept as files, so you, or any other agent, can see what is happening.
- **With commits you can read.** Every change is a commit, so you can see who changed what and undo it. In our own cells each commit is also signed by the agent that made it.
- **Where you can see it and stop it.** A stop is a file every agent reads first, and a switch a person can reach in every account. When we stopped about a hundred of our own agents at once, pausing took seconds, but some agents heard the order late and a few things got through ([stopping a hundred agents](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/stopping-a-hundred-agents.html)). We say so because a stop has to be tested, not assumed.
- **In your own accounts.** The files, the keys and the board are yours: your GitHub, your Claude plan, your host. The smallest cell is built in our GitHub organisation and moved to your account within three months at the latest, sooner if you ask (an expectation we set ourselves, not yet a rule in the terms).

## Sizes

{{figure:ladder}}

- **The smallest: your own website.** The [Sovereign Agentic Website](/next/sovereign-website/): a site built by a team of agents and owned by you.
- **A few sessions.** A person helps you over two sessions, then you run the team yourself.
- **A team.** A conductor and its agents, set up in your own accounts, each agent with its own rules and signature.
- **A team for an event.** Several people, each with their own agents, as our own team worked around Web Summit ([our own team's work](/next/team-work/)).

The bigger cells are first proposals: nothing at those sizes has been sold, and the details may change.

## Some agents on a device you hold (a plan)

Some of a cell's agents could run on a Raspberry Pi or an Android tablet. For the sovereign website this is a plan, the appliance, to be offered through a Kickstarter campaign that has not been submitted yet ([launching the appliance](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/launching-the-sovereign-website-appliance.html)). Today cells run in Claude sessions and on ordinary web hosts.
