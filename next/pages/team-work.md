# Bigger cells: our own team at work

An agentic cell is a small team of AI agents, each with a role, a brief and a behaviour policy, working in your own accounts, where you can see the work and stop it ([what a cell is](/next/cells/)). A website is one of the simplest jobs a cell can do. Bigger cells do more: they run a newsroom, a team's working site or an event, the work our [team and network sizes](/next/pricing/) are for. This page shows that kind of work, from our own team's work; [PrompTugal.news](/next/case-studies/), one of our case studies, came out of it. It names no customer: this was our own work.

## Ten days around Web Summit

Between 3 and 13 November 2026, six people and around 250 named agents built a working site for the team, a newsroom, a site about a storm, a guide to the summit's fringe, a draft rating standard and native apps ([ten days at Web Summit](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/ten-days-at-web-summit.html)). Each person had their own agents and their own conductor, and every commit says whose agent made it.

The counts depend on how you count: "around 250 agents" is the number of distinct agent names in the commits, and sessions are not agents ([the same article](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/ten-days-at-web-summit.html)).

## What it made, in public

- **The team's working site:** a brief and a command centre for each person, a key and an inbox for each agent, the board as files ([web-summit.sgit.ai](https://web-summit.sgit.ai/), and [the team's operating system](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/the-teams-operating-system.html)).
- **A newsroom:** our own newsroom on technology and AI in Portugal, run with desks of agents and reviewers ([PrompTugal.news](https://promptugal.news/), and [the newsroom we ran](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/promptugal-news-the-newsroom-we-ran.html)).
- **A site about a storm,** built while the storm reached Lisbon ([Storm Charles](https://storm-charles.promptugal.news/)).
- **A guide to the summit's fringe** ([Lisbon Tech Fringe](https://lisbontechfringe.com/)).
- **Native apps,** for iPhone, iPad and an Android tablet ([native apps by agents](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/native-apps-by-agents.html)).

## Seen, and stopped

A bigger cell has to be stoppable, and we tested it. When we told about a hundred agents to stop, pausing took seconds, but each conductor only heard the order at its next wake, and a few things got through ([stopping a hundred agents](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/stopping-a-hundred-agents.html)). The lesson we took: a stop is not a message, it is a file every agent reads first, and a switch a person can reach in every account.
