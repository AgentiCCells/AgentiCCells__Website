## Can you leave?

This is the row that matters most: a platform earns every month because you stay. [No lock-in](/next/no-lock-in/) explains why we put it first.

## Where the others are better

- **One bill for editor, hosting and support.** With us, you edit with your own Claude (a paid plan) or through a partner, and you choose the host.
- **Online shops and memberships.** Wix, Squarespace and Shopify run stores, payments and log-ins. A static site does not, unless you add a service for it.
- **Apps for organisations.** Fliplet builds apps with sign-in for large organisations; for them a platform may well be right, as Dinis Cruz says in [rent or own](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/fliplet-and-the-website-cell.html).
- **A person you can phone.** A freelancer gives you a person. A partner can give you that around a site you own.

## Moving from a platform

If you already have a site, it is the material for the new one. WordPress.com exports posts, pages and comments, but not your theme, customisations or plugins, and links to your images rather than the files ([WordPress.com's export guide](https://wordpress.com/support/export/), read 19 November 2026). Squarespace exports only certain content, in WordPress's format ([Squarespace's export guide](https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/206566687-Export-your-site-content), read 19 November 2026). Wix says its sites must run on its own servers, but the public pages of any site can be the starting point. An automatic converter is a proposal, not something that exists yet.
