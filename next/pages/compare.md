## Where the others are better

A fair comparison says this too:

- **The platforms bundle an editor, hosting and support** in one bill. With us, you edit with your own Claude, which needs a Claude plan, or through a partner, and hosting is yours to choose.
- **Online shops and memberships.** Platforms such as Wix, Squarespace and Shopify run stores, payments and log-ins for you. A static site does not, unless you add a service for it.
- **Apps for organisations.** Fliplet builds web and mobile apps with authentication and single sign-on for large organisations; for those buyers a platform may well be right, as Dinis Cruz says in [rent or own](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/fliplet-and-the-website-cell.html).
- **A person you can phone.** A freelancer or agency gives you a person. A partner can give you that around a site you own.

For a person or a small business who wants a site that is theirs, owning it is the better shape.

## Moving from a platform

If you already have a site, it is the material: the interview asks what you would change, and the five designs are better versions of it. WordPress can export everything, and Squarespace exports part of a site in WordPress's format; Wix does not export, but its public pages can be read as the starting point ([static against the platforms](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/static-against-the-platforms.html)). A converter that does this automatically is a proposal, not something that exists yet.
