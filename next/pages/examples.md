# Look at the sites first

This page is about the Sovereign Agentic Website, one of our two [case studies](/next/case-studies/). A cell is a small team of AI agents, each with a role, a brief and written rules, working in your own accounts ([what a cell is](/next/cells/)); for a bigger cell at work, see [our own team's work](/next/team-work/).

A website you own, control and can take anywhere starts as a site you would want. These are sites made by the same team of agents, so you can judge the work before you ask for anything.

{{examples}}

## How to read this page

- **Named people** agreed to be named, and we record that consent before a name appears.
- **Demos** are sites we made to show what the team can do. They are labelled as demos, and anything invented in them, such as a company, its prices or its orders, says it is invented.
- **None is a sale.** Nothing has been sold yet.

## What the examples show

Each person's site was made in five designs, and the person chose. Two of the stories, in Dinis Cruz's words:

- [A website that is her](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/a-website-that-is-her.html): Yiheng Wang's site, made in one evening, and changed afterwards by her own Claude from her CV.
- [A portfolio with a film first](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/a-portfolio-with-a-film-first.html): Silvio Switha's site, with his music video on the first screen.

Every site we have made is also listed in [our catalogue of sites](https://hub.agenticcells.site/), with its repository.
