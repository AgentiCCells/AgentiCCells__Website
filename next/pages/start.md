# Start with an interview

A cell is a small team of AI agents, each with a role, a brief and written rules, working in your own accounts ([what a cell is](/next/cells/)). Whatever the size, it starts the same way: say what the work is, in your own words.

## Asking for a cell

Write down the one job you want done, who does it today, and what must stay private. Say which of the [four sizes](/next/pricing/) you think fits, from one agent for one workflow to a network; if you are not sure, say so, and a person will suggest one. Every price is a first proposal, and nothing has been sold yet.

## Asking for a Sovereign Website

The rest of this page is about the Sovereign Agentic Website, one of our two [case studies](/next/case-studies/), which has its own launch offer on [sovereignwebsite.ai](https://sovereignwebsite.ai/) (opening soon).

A website you own, control and can take anywhere starts with a conversation. Most people do not know what they want from a website until someone asks them the right questions, so the first step is an interview, by Claude.

## How to ask

Three steps:

1. **Run the interview** in your own Claude, with the prompt below. It writes a one-page brief, in your words.
2. **Gather your material:** photos, videos, documents, a CV, or links to what is already public, such as a profile or an existing site. Note which of it may be public.
3. **Ask for a site**, at the Sovereign Website's launch price or as a free evaluation, and send the brief and the material.

To ask now, use the contact at the foot of any page of the [live site](https://agenticcells.ai/). This preview has no address of its own yet. A person reads every request and replies.

## The interview, in your own Claude

Paste this into your own Claude. It asks you one question at a time and writes a one-page brief, in your words. The prompt itself sends nothing anywhere: the brief stays with you until you choose to send it.

```
Interview me, one question at a time, to write the brief for my website.
Cover: what the site is for and who should read it; the sections I want;
the photos, videos and documents I have; three websites I like and what
I like about each; what to avoid and what must stay private.
Ask me which of my material may be public, and do not name any other
person in the brief unless I say they have agreed to be named.
Then write the brief on one page, in my words, and ask me to correct it.
```

The first five lines are Dinis Cruz's, from [start with an interview](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/start-with-an-interview.html); we added the two lines on what may be public and on naming other people. An interview on this site, with the same questions, is planned.

The question that matters most is the sites you like. "I like this site's photographs, that one's calm, and the way this one tells a story" is a better design brief than most designers get. The five designs take the qualities you name; they never copy someone else's site.

## Already have a site?

Then you have already answered most of the questions. Send a link to it: its words, photos and structure are the material, the interview asks what you would change, and the five designs are better versions of what you have.

## The free evaluation

We build a limited number of sites each day free of charge, for evaluation. It is the way to see before you pay: when you ask, say you would like a free evaluation. We publish only what you say may be public.
