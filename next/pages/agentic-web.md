# The agentic web

The agentic web is sites and services run by teams of AI agents, for the people who own them. We call each such team an agentic cell: a small team of agents, each with a role, a brief and a behaviour policy, working in the owner's own accounts, where the owner can see the work and stop it ([what a cell is](/next/cells/)).

## What is different from the web of 2000

In Dinis Cruz's words: "In 2000 the open web gave you software to run yourself; WordPress made publishing free, but you or someone you paid still did the work. In 2026 a team of agents does the work: it interviews you, builds the site, shows you designs, and afterwards your own AI changes it when you ask." ([Sovereign Agentic Website](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/sovereign-agentic-website.html), 19 November 2026.)

And: "The agents work inside a behaviour policy that says what they may and may not touch, so the site stays yours while they work on it." ([the same article](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/sovereign-agentic-website.html).)

## Owned, not rented

An agentic web only helps the owner if the owner holds it. That is why every cell we sell keeps its files, its history and its rules in the owner's accounts, and why the code is open source. Sovereignty, in Dinis's words, "is not where your data is hosted but whether you can move it" ([sovereign agentic website](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/sovereign-agentic-website.html)).

## A team, not a tool

"A website builder gives you tools; an agency gives you people at an hourly rate. This gives you a team, for one payment, that you own." ([a website with a team inside](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/a-website-with-a-team-inside.html), 19 November 2026.) The team of six or seven agents inside a site, which that article describes, is the next step, not yet built: the sites we hand over today carry instructions and a behaviour policy for one agent, the owner's own Claude.

## How the pieces stack

Dinis describes the stack in four layers ([designing sovereignwebsite.ai](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/designing-sovereignwebsite-ai.html)):

- **SGit:** encrypted, versioned vaults, where work and its history live ([sgit.ai](https://sgit.ai/)).
- **RiskMandate:** the behaviour policies that bound what agents do ([riskmandate.ai](https://riskmandate.ai/)).
- **AgentiCCells:** agentic cells, set up for a customer.
- **Sovereign Website:** a website built by agents and owned by you, the first layer a small business can buy without knowing the others exist ([sovereignwebsite.ai](https://sovereignwebsite.ai/), opening soon).
