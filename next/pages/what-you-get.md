# What you get

This page is about the smallest cell, the sovereign website. A cell is a small team of AI agents, each with a role, a brief and written rules, working in your own accounts ([what a cell is](/next/cells/)).

A website you own, control and can take anywhere. This page shows what that means in practice: what arrives, where it lives, and what you can do with it.

## The repository is the site

Everything else follows from one fact: the site is a set of files in a GitHub repository, and the repository is in your name. It holds the pages, the pictures, the five designs, the instructions for your Claude and the rules for any agent, with every change since the first. Whoever holds the repository holds the site.

We build it in our GitHub organisation, then move it to your own account within three months at the latest, sooner if you ask; until then we hold it for you. That is why we can promise the rest. You can host it anywhere because it is plain files. You can change it with your own Claude because the instructions are in the repository. Once it is moved, you keep it even if we disappear, because nothing of yours is left with us.

## What the five designs are for

People find it hard to describe a design and easy to point at one. So the team makes five, from your material and the qualities you named in the sites you like, and you choose. The other four are kept in the repository, so you can switch later. See [how it works](/next/how-it-works/) for where the designs fit.

## The CLAUDE.md and the policy

Two short files help keep changes to the site safe:

- **CLAUDE.md**, written for you: what the site is, how to change words and photos, which checks run, and what not to touch. Your Claude reads it before it changes anything.
- **A behaviour policy**: what any agent working on the site may and may not do. The checks (a build, a validation and a leak scan) run before anything goes live, and any change can be undone.

A person who knows what they are doing can override them. Most owners will never want to.

## Ownership within three months

We expect to transfer every site we build to its owner within three months, sooner if you ask: the repository moves to your GitHub account and the address points where you choose. We hold it only to finish the work. This is an expectation we set ourselves, not yet a rule in the terms ([the reasoning, in Dinis Cruz's words](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/fliplet-and-the-website-cell.html)).

## What you do not get from us

- **A subscription.** There is none.
- **Support after hand-over.** Our part ends when the site is yours. Your own Claude, or a [partner](/next/partners/), looks after it.
- **An editor in a browser.** You edit by talking to Claude, which needs your own Claude plan (Claude Pro is USD 17 a month billed yearly, [Anthropic's page](https://claude.com/pricing), read 19 November 2026), or a partner who edits for you. If nobody edits it, the site keeps running as it is.
