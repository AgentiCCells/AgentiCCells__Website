# The sovereign website: the smallest cell

Built by agents, owned by you. An agentic cell is a small team of AI agents, each with a role, a brief and a behaviour policy, working in your own accounts ([what a cell is](/next/cells/)). The smallest cell we sell, and the clearest example of what a cell does, is the Sovereign Agentic Website (Sovereign Website for short): a website built by a team of agents and owned by you, the files, the history and the address, in a repository you control.

Its own site is [sovereignwebsite.ai](https://sovereignwebsite.ai/) (opening soon).

## What it is

- **Built by agents.** A team of agents interviews you, makes five designs from your material, and puts the chosen one live with HTTPS.
- **Owned by you.** The site is plain files in a GitHub repository. We build it in our GitHub organisation, then move it, with its full history, to your own account within three months at the latest, sooner if you ask. The three months is an expectation we set ourselves, not yet a rule in the terms.
- **Changed by talking.** You ask your own Claude, the AI assistant, for a change in plain words. Checks run before anything goes live, and every change can be undone.
- **Movable.** Any web host can run it, in any region you choose, and nobody can lock you in, including us.
- **An open pattern.** Like WordPress, anyone can use the pattern without paying us ([Sovereign Agentic Website](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/sovereign-agentic-website.html)).

## The team inside (the next step)

The plan is that a sovereign website comes with a team inside its repository: six or seven agents already set up and coordinated ([a website with a team inside](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/a-website-with-a-team-inside.html)).

- **Conductor:** the one you talk to. It turns a request into a plan, hands out the work, and asks before anything goes live.
- **Designer:** layouts and images, in the style you chose.
- **Writer:** the words, in your voice, from what you say and send.
- **Developer:** builds the change, keeps the checks passing, and publishes once you approve.
- **Reviewer:** reads every change before you see it: prices, claims, privacy, and anyone named without consent.
- **Translator:** keeps every page in each of the site's languages.
- **Keeper:** the weekly care: broken links, speed, accessibility, the certificate and the domain's renewal.

This team inside the site is the next step, not yet built. The sites we hand over today carry a CLAUDE.md and a behaviour policy for one agent, your own Claude. The team would run on your own Claude account, at its cost and within its limits.

## On a device you hold (a plan)

Some of these agents could run on a Raspberry Pi or an Android tablet. The appliance is a plan: a configured Android tablet, or a set of Raspberry Pis that serve the site from home, to be offered through a Kickstarter campaign. Nothing has been submitted to Kickstarter, and the appliance's software is a plan ([launching the appliance](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/launching-the-sovereign-website-appliance.html)). The site on its own, without a device, is what we sell now.
