# How it works

This page is about the smallest cell, the sovereign website. A cell is a small team of AI agents, each with a role, a brief and written rules, working in your own accounts ([what a cell is](/next/cells/)).

From asking for a site to changing it yourself by talking to Claude, the AI assistant. There are seven steps, and the one that matters most is the first change you make yourself.

{{figure:how-it-works}}

## The steps

### Ask for a site

[Ask for a site]({{contact}}) at the launch price, or apply for a free evaluation site. A person reads every request and replies.

### The interview

It starts with Claude interviewing you, one question at a time. It covers five things: what the site is for and who should read it; the sections you want; the material you have (photos, videos, documents, a CV, an existing site); three sites you like and why; and what to avoid and what must stay private. It ends with a one-page brief, in your words, that you read and correct.

Dinis Cruz's reason for starting here, from [start with an interview](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/start-with-an-interview.html): "Most people do not know what they want from a website until someone asks them the right questions."

Today you run the interview in your own Claude, with the prompt on the [start page](/next/start/); an interview on this site is planned. Your material can then come in whichever way suits you: an email with attachments, a zip, or links to what is already public.

If you already have a site, it is the material. The interview asks what you would change, and the five designs are better versions of what you have.

### Five designs

A team of AI agents makes five designs from your material. They take the qualities you named in the sites you like, such as the photographs, the calm, the way a story is told, never a copy of someone else's site.

### You choose

You pick one, or ask for parts of two, and say what to change. The other designs stay in the repository.

### Live

The site goes live at its address, with HTTPS, once the checks pass: a build, a validation and a leak scan. Until you have your own domain, it runs on an address of ours.

### Your first change, made together

This is the milestone. You ask your own Claude for a change (a sentence, a photo, a new job from your CV) and it goes live while we both watch. Until then you have a website; from then on you have what we sell.

### Hand-over

The repository moves to your GitHub account, with its full history, and the address points where you choose. We expect this within three months at the latest, sooner if you ask: an expectation we set ourselves, not yet a rule in the terms. After that we hold nothing.

## Who does the work

{{figure:team}}

Even the smallest site is made by seven roles, each played by an AI agent with a written job description and a behaviour policy, each signing its work. For a small site, the figure above says what each one does.

A person approves what matters. The agents draft; they never send email, post, or pay for anything on anyone's behalf.

## What this page does not claim

- No site has been sold yet, so no order has been timed. We give an honest estimate when we reply.
- The interview on this site is planned; today it runs in your own Claude.
- The three-month hand-over is an expectation we set ourselves, not yet a rule in the terms.
