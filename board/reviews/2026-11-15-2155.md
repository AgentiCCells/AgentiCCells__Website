Dark mode holds up: contrast reads well, cards and the seven-step diagram work in both themes. The honest wording ("nothing has been sold yet", "first proposal") is the site's strongest point.

## The three biggest problems
1. **Navigation** (all pages, both widths). Nine text links wrap to two lines on desktop and three on a phone. The mark "AgentiCCells" sits directly above a nav item also called "AgentiCCells", so the name appears twice. "How this site was built", "Case study", "The console" and "the-console" are separate pages a buyer cannot tell apart. Dinis saw this on /about/ at 21:45.
2. **Plain, text-only look** (home and most pages). The logo is an outlined square beside plain text; the home page is a column of black text on white with one button, "Email agent@riskmandate.ai". The main offer is a bold underlined text link below it, not a button. There is no hero, no picture of a cell, no sense of a product. It reads as a draft, not a professional business. (Only /website-cell/ has a diagram.)
3. **Heavy, repeated links and stale console text** (home, /about/, /open-source/, /console/). Paragraphs carry "(source)" and "Link" links: bare words with no meaning out of context, and many are under 32 px tall. On /console/ the board repeats status text such as "Done (2026-11-15, v0.1.0 live at https://agenticcells.com)" and "seven pages live at https://agenticcells.com", although the site is on agenticcells.ai; the page is also 57,812 px long on a phone. The stale text hurts trust on the page that is meant to show trust.

## Other things seen
- Home at 1280 px: text column is narrow and left-aligned with wide empty margins; fine to read, flat to look at.
- /key/ is in the sitemap but not in the nav (only linked from the website cell page); the page is a prototype and says little about why a buyer would use it.
- No page shows a screenshot, a number or a named result (correctly: none exists yet). A "what you would see" mock-up marked as a mock-up would help without claiming a sale.
