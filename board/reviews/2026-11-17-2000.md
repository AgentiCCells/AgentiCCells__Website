## The three biggest problems
1. **The offer is not the headline.** The default home opens on a team photo and a definition. The website cell is one sentence on the second screen. D-SELL and W-SELL1 cover this; queued items must keep the price and "not yet sold" honest until Dinis decides.
2. **No way to say yes.** One email address, no list of what to send, no next step, no partner route; /partners/ and /deploy/ are 404. P-SELL and W-PARTNERS cover the path; RV items below add "what you send" and a pre-launch check.
3. **/website-cell/ describes the old flow.** "No accounts of your own", hourly signed requests and three months of hosting conflict with the strategy (own repository, own Claude or any host, no support from us). The rewrite (W-SELL2) must reconcile these before it goes live, or Dinis's words and the page disagree.

## Other problems

5. "from £500" next to "£500, one-off" on /website-cell/.
