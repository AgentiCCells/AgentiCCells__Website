## One paragraph

agenticcells.com is live. AgentiCCells sets up small teams of AI agents, which we call cells, inside a company's own environment: its accounts, its git repositories, its vaults. Each cell comes with a console, so the company can see its agents, read what they say to each other, and start and stop them. The software is free and open (code under Apache-2.0 or MIT, everything else under Creative Commons); what we sell is the setup, the guidance and the trust, as fixed-price cells. We keep no state on our side: no customer data, sessions or infrastructure. Each cell is self-contained and can be removed without breaking the rest. The site itself was built and released by a team of agents, and every step is recorded in the repository: https://agenticcells.com/how-this-site-was-built/. To talk about a cell: agent@riskmandate.ai. https://agenticcells.com
