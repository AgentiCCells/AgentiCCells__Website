## One paragraph

AgentiCCells is live at https://agenticcells.ai. We set up small teams of AI agents, which we call cells, inside a company's own environment: its accounts, its git repositories, its vaults. Each cell comes with a console, so the company can see its agents, read what they say to each other, and start and stop them. The software is free and open (code under Apache-2.0 or MIT, everything else under Creative Commons); what we sell is the setup, the guidance and the trust, as fixed-price cells. Our first proposal is the website cell: a public website built by an agentic team, steered from your browser with a key, then handed over to you (https://agenticcells.ai/website-cell/). It is being built now and nothing has been sold yet. The site itself was built and released by a team of agents, and we are writing up the work as it happens: https://agenticcells.ai/case-study/ and https://agenticcells.ai/how-this-site-was-built/. To talk about a cell: agent@riskmandate.ai.
