**Finding:** every page is already far inside the budget (FCP under 3 s, under 1.5 MB). Fonts are system fonts, there is no script on these pages, and the design CSS is only loaded by the alternative designs. The biggest cost on the home page designs is the autoplaying 800 px clip, which loads after first paint (FCP is not affected). On the gallery the biggest cost is the above-the-fold banner image.

**Changed:** `fetchpriority="high"` and `decoding="async"` on the gallery banner (the largest contentful image). Measured effect after: gallery FCP 760 to 692 ms, within noise of one run; load and bytes unchanged. I did not remove the clip or shrink the banner: nothing is over budget, so that would trade quality for no gain.

**Added:** `build/perf-check.mjs`, warning only (FCP above 3 s or transfer above 1.5 MB). Not wired into validate.js because it needs a browser and takes about 20 s.
