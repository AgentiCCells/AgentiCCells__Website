# For freelancers: sell a website, an agentic email and a workflow

You are one person who talks to people, and you would like to sell more of what they ask you for. This page is for you, and you can read it without any other. Nothing has been sold yet, through a partner or otherwise, and no partner has signed. [Email us as a freelancer](mailto:agent@riskmandate.ai?subject=I%20might%20be%20a%20freelancer%20partner) when you want to talk.

## What you sell

You can say to a customer: "for this amount, I will give you a website, an agentic email and a workflow." You focus on one kind of customer and one flow, and you do more of it. What stands behind your offer is the [website cell](/website-cell/): a public site built by a team of agents from the customer's photos, videos and words, in a GitHub repository the customer owns.

The model is Dinis Cruz's, in his words: "the rules of engagement here are 50-50 split ... if they bring the business, I do the automation, I do the flow, I set that up, and then they can do whatever they want" ([his brief](https://github.com/AgentiCCells/AgentiCCells__Website/blob/dev/board/briefs/website-cell-sales-2026-11-17.md), 2026-11-17). The full programme is in his article, [Partners sell, we build](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/partners-sell-we-build.html) (CC BY 4.0).

## A worked example, from first conversation to hand-over and after

{{partner-fig:flow:freelancer}}

This is an example of how a deal would run, not a record of one: no deal has run.

1. **First conversation.** A customer tells you they need a site and have no time for it. You agree what they want and a price. The website cell is {{price}} from us, or you set your own.
2. **Material.** They send you photos, videos and a few paragraphs, and say what may be public. You send it to us at agent@riskmandate.ai.
3. **Set up.** The factory makes the repository, the address, the CLAUDE.md and the behaviour policy. The agent team makes five designs.
4. **Choose.** You show the customer the previews. They choose and say what to change.
5. **Hand-over.** The repository moves to the customer's GitHub with its full history, and you are given admin access if they agree.
6. **After.** You host it, support it, change it and charge for it as you like. You may also sell them an agentic email or a workflow, and we come back for new features and templates when you ask.

## The money

{{partner-fig:money:freelancer}}

- **50/50 of the sale** when we are involved. Whoever has the customer invoices them and pays the other half.
- **Payment terms are agreed per deal.**
- **Hosting, support and retainers are yours entirely.** We take no part and no share, and we sell no subscriptions.
- **Prices are yours.** The website cell is {{price}}. The ladder above it, a first proposal, is [£1,500, £5,000 and £20,000](https://github.com/AgentiCCells/AgentiCCells__Website/blob/dev/data/cells.json) (data/cells.json). You may use it or set your own prices. The split is on what the customer pays for the build.
- Our part is the set-up, then new features, configurations, workflows, templates and behaviour policies, each on the same split.

The figures and rules come from [Dinis Cruz's article](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/partners-sell-we-build.html); where the page and the article differ on the website cell's price, [data/offer.json](https://github.com/AgentiCCells/AgentiCCells__Website/blob/dev/data/offer.json) wins, because Dinis set it after the article.

## What you need to start

A customer, or the wish to find one; an email address; a GitHub account for the customer to receive the site (we can point you to guidance). You need no technical skill and no agent of your own.

## Partner terms

The terms are common sense and few. The customer owns the site, the repository and the material. You own the relationship. Our code and policies are [open](/open-source/), and you may keep your own repositories and your customers' work private. Nobody is named publicly, customer or partner, without agreement. It works in any country and any language, and you invoice in your own currency under your own law. The same terms are on the [partners page](/partners/#partner-terms).
