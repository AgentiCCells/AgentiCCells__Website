# For consultancies: one cell for each client, the same team every time

You run a small company with customers, and some of them would want this. This page is for you, and you can read it without any other. [Email us as a consultancy](mailto:agent@riskmandate.ai?subject=I%20might%20be%20a%20consultancy%20partner) when you want to talk.

## What you sell

A website cell for each client, then the workflows behind it. Because the set-up is automated, a second site is not a second project: it is the same team and the same policies with a new customer's material. Even the smallest cell is a team of agents, a conductor, a researcher, a designer, a developer and a security reviewer, each with a job description and a behaviour policy ([source](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/partners-sell-we-build.html)). You are selling a team, not a template.

The model is Dinis Cruz's, in his words: "the rules of engagement here are 50-50 split ... if they bring the business, I do the automation, I do the flow, I set that up, and then they can do whatever they want" ([his brief](https://github.com/AgentiCCells/AgentiCCells__Website/blob/dev/board/briefs/website-cell-sales-2026-11-17.md), 2026-11-17). The full programme is in his article, [Partners sell, we build](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/partners-sell-we-build.html) (CC BY 4.0).

## A worked example, from first conversation to hand-over and after

{{partner-fig:flow:consultancy}}

One of your clients asks for a site, or you offer one. You collect their photos, videos and words, and which parts may be public, and send them to us. The factory sets up the cell, the agent team makes five designs, and you and the client choose. The repository goes to the client's GitHub with its history and you get admin access. You run hosting, support and retainers at your prices, and the second client repeats the same steps with the same team. This is an example of how a deal would run, not a record of one: no deal has run.

## The money

{{partner-fig:money:consultancy}}

- **50/50 of the sale** when we are involved. Whoever has the customer invoices them and pays the other half.
- **Payment terms are agreed per deal.**
- **Hosting, support and retainers are yours entirely.** We take no part and no share, and we sell no subscriptions.
- **Prices are yours.** The website cell is {{price}}. The ladder above it, a first proposal, is [£1,500, £5,000 and £20,000](https://github.com/AgentiCCells/AgentiCCells__Website/blob/dev/data/cells.json) (data/cells.json). You may use it or set your own prices. The split is on what the customer pays for the build.
- Our part is the set-up, then new features, configurations, workflows, templates and behaviour policies, each on the same split.

The figures and rules come from [Dinis Cruz's article](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/partners-sell-we-build.html); where the page and the article differ on the website cell's price, [data/offer.json](https://github.com/AgentiCCells/AgentiCCells__Website/blob/dev/data/offer.json) wins, because Dinis set it after the article.

## What you need to start

A client list or a way to reach it; one person at your company who sends us material and talks to clients; the clients' GitHub accounts. You decide how you price and what you promise.

## Partner terms

Nothing has been sold yet, through a partner or otherwise, and no partner has signed. The terms are common sense and few. The customer owns the site, the repository and the material. You own the relationship. Our code and policies are [open](/open-source/), and you may keep your own repositories and your customers' work private. Nobody is named publicly, customer or partner, without agreement. It works in any country and any language, and you invoice in your own currency under your own law. The same terms are on the [partners page](/partners/#partner-terms).
