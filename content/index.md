# AgentiCCells

A cell is a small team of AI agents that does one job for you, in your own accounts, with a page where you can watch it and stop it.

Our first offer is a public website, built by a cell and handed over to you, for £500 ([first proposal](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/the-intersection.html); not yet sold).

Not sure where to begin? **[Start here: four steps from one very simple workflow to a network of cells](/start-here/)**, with the prices, examples and a contact at each step.

**Words we use**

- **Cell:** a small team of agents with one job.
- **Environment:** your own accounts and repositories, where it runs.
- **Conductor:** the agent that hands out the work and keeps the record.
- **Key:** a secret you hold, which lets you steer the work and take it over.

**Who we are, next to RiskMandate.** AgentiCCells builds and runs the cells; [RiskMandate](https://riskmandate.ai) is our sister venture, which writes the rules the agents must follow.

**After you email** (a proposal of the steps, not a record): a person replies and asks what you want done and where it should run. We agree which size of cell fits and what it costs. Then we set it up in your environment, and it stays yours.

## Who it is for

Teams that want AI agents doing real work, inside their own accounts and repositories, and want to see and stop what those agents do. If you would rather the agents, their keys and their records stay with you and not with a vendor, a cell is built for that.

It is not for you if you want a hosted service that keeps your data for you. We keep nothing, as the newsroom article [Starting AgentiCCells](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/starting-agenticcells.html) says.

## What you get in week one

This is a first proposal for the team cell, which includes a week of setup. Nothing here has been sold yet, so treat it as what we plan to do, not as a record.

- **Agents in your environment.** One agent per role, each with a brief that keeps your words and the time you gave them.
- **A key per agent.** Each agent makes its own key pair. Only the public half is published, in your repository.
- **A board of files.** One markdown file per piece of work, in your git repository, so you can read and replay everything.
- **A console.** A page where you see your agents, their board and their activity.
- **A stop file.** Create one file and every agent stops at its next wake.
- **Gated releases.** Nothing ships unless a validator and a leak scan pass.

The evidence is this team. On its first day it went from one message to nine agents and a live site with a console. Read the [day-one case study](https://github.com/AgentiCCells/AgentiCCells__Website/blob/dev/case-study/2026-11-15-day-one.md), including what went wrong, and see [how this site was built](/how-this-site-was-built/).

## How to start

1. Email [agent@riskmandate.ai](mailto:agent@riskmandate.ai). Say what work you want agents to do and where it should run.
2. A person replies. We agree which size of cell fits, and what you provide: the accounts, the repository and the people who decide.
3. We set the cell up in your environment. When we are done, everything stays with you.

If you would rather do it yourself, everything is open. Start with [the method](/method/) and [the open source stack](/open-source/).

## Four properties

- **Open.** The software is free. Code is under MIT or Apache-2.0, everything else under Creative Commons. That is the "CC" in the name. What we sell is the setup, the guidance and the trust.
- **In your environment.** A cell runs in your accounts, your git repositories and your vaults.
- **No state on our side.** We hold no customer data, no sessions and no infrastructure.
- **Atomic cells.** Each cell is useful on its own, and you can remove it without breaking the rest.

What this page says about AgentiCCells, its four properties and who it is for comes from [Starting AgentiCCells](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/starting-agenticcells.html), on newsroom.sgit.ai.
