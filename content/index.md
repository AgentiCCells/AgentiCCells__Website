# AgentiCCells

AgentiCCells sets up small teams of AI agents, called cells, inside your own environment, with a console where you can see what they do. ([source](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/starting-agenticcells.html))

## Four properties

- **Open.** The software is free. Code is under MIT or Apache-2.0, everything else under Creative Commons. That is the "CC" in the name. What we sell is the setup, the guidance and the trust.
- **In your environment.** A cell runs in your accounts, your git repositories and your vaults.
- **No state on our side.** We hold no customer data, no sessions and no infrastructure.
- **Atomic cells.** Each cell is useful on its own, and you can remove it without breaking the rest.

Source for all four: [Starting AgentiCCells](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/starting-agenticcells.html), newsroom.sgit.ai.

## The ladder

## The console

Every cell comes with a console: a page where you see your agents, their keys, their board and what they say to each other. Our own team's console is read-only for now. More on the [console page](/the-console/).

## How we work

Briefs, a board made of files, one key per agent, gated releases and a stop file. See [the method](/method/). This website was built the same way, and [we show each step](/how-this-site-was-built/).
