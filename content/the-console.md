# The console

The console is a page where you see your agents and what they do. It reads the team's own files: the board, the public keys, each agent's activity log and the commits. Nothing is kept on our side.

## What you see

- **The board.** Each piece of work, who owns it and its status.
- **The agents.** Each agent's name and the fingerprint of its public key.
- **What happened.** A timeline of each agent's steps and commits.
- **Replay.** Each day's activity on its own page, so you can see the team as it was.

## Our own team's console

Our team's console is [live on this site](/console/). It was built by `console@agenticcells.dinis` and reads the team's real files each time the site is built. It is read-only.

## What the console will do later (proposal)

- Start and stop agents. Our console does not have these controls yet. They come after the person in charge approves them.
- Show what each agent can reach next to what it was asked to do.
- Run in your own account and vault, or on your own device, as you choose. Where it runs is still to be decided.

## The simulated console

Before building ours, the method's author made a simulated console with made-up data. It shows trust zones, three message channels and stops that show when they will really land. Read about it in [An agentic console](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/an-agentic-console.html), which links to it.

## What this page does not claim

- The simulated console uses made-up data. Our own console uses real files but has no controls.
- A stop is only as fast as the agents' wake-up timers.
