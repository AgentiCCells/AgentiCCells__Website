# Cells

A cell is a small agentic team set up in your environment. It is useful on its own, and you can remove it without breaking anything else. There are four sizes.

## Two modes: who runs the team

Any cell can be run in one of two ways. Both come from Dinis Cruz, [Atomic cells, end to end](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/atomic-cells-end-to-end.html), newsroom.sgit.ai, 2026-11-15. Prices are a first proposal, not an offer.

- **No-touch: we run the team.** £500, first proposal. The agentic team runs on our accounts and builds for you. You need only a browser. This is the [website cell](/website-cell/): you steer with a key, and at handover the repository moves to your GitHub.
- **Their own: they run the team.** From £1,500, first proposal. The team runs in your own GitHub and Claude accounts, which are set up in a couple of meetings. We then step away. It costs more and needs those accounts, and you hold the access.

What differs is whose accounts the work runs in. In the second mode we keep no state, because the work never ran on our side.

Neither mode has been sold yet. The end-to-end flow for the first is being built now.

## The ladder as biology

The newsroom reads the ladder as biology ([Atomic cells, end to end](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/atomic-cells-end-to-end.html), Dinis Cruz, newsroom.sgit.ai, 2026-11-15). Its words are quoted below. Every price is a first proposal, not an offer.

- **Single cell.** "One site, one small team, a feedback lane and a handover." £500, first proposal. This is the no-touch mode above.
- **Cell handed over.** "Set up with the customer in a couple of meetings and handed over into their own accounts." From £1,500, first proposal. This is the second mode above.
- **Colony.** "Several cells integrated with the customer's work, with a conductor, a board and keys." £5,000, first proposal.
- **Multi-cell organism.** "A custom network of cells, conductors of conductors, policies and observability." £20,000, first proposal. The article's caption calls this stage "a network".

The article's rungs and the four sizes above are not a perfect match. Its £1,500 rung (first proposal) is a single cell handed over, while the £1,500 size above (first proposal) is a few sessions. We have not reconciled the two. Neither has been sold yet, and the biology is the newsroom's way of reading the ladder, not a measured result.

## The six setups of an agentic system

The newsroom describes six setups, from the simplest up ([source](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/six-setups-of-an-agentic-system.html)):

- A task.
- One agent.
- An agent with sub-agents, in one runtime.
- Several sessions, one per role.
- A conductor and its agents.
- Conductors of conductors.

The lower three are a prompt. The upper three are a setup, with keys, lanes, boards and policies.

## Which cell matches which setup

Our reading of the ladder against the six setups:

- **One agent** sets up an agent with sub-agents in one runtime.
- **A few sessions** sets up several sessions, one per role.
- **A team** sets up a conductor and its agents.
- **A network** sets up conductors of conductors.

## What every cell includes

- The console, so you can see the agents and what they do.
- A key per agent, with only the public half published.
- A board of files in your own repository.
- No state on our side. When the work is done, everything stays with you.
