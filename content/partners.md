# Partners: you bring the business, we build the site

You know people who want a website. We have an agent team that builds one from photos, videos and a short description, and hands it over in a GitHub repository the customer owns. You can sell that to them, with us building it behind you.

**These terms are a first draft. Talk to us.** No partner agreement exists yet, and nothing here is a contract. Every price and every split on this page is a proposal. The first partner to agree will shape the final terms. Nothing has been sold yet, through a partner or otherwise.

## What a partner sells

The [website cell](/website-cell/) and the agent team behind it. Even the simplest cell is several agents wired up, each with a job description and a behaviour policy, working on a board you can read. You are selling a team, not a template. How the team works is on the [method page](/method/), and the example sites are on [the hub](https://hub.agenticcells.site/).

## The deal, in Dinis Cruz's terms

Dinis Cruz, 2026-11-17, in his brief for this work ([board/briefs/website-cell-sales-2026-11-17.md](https://github.com/AgentiCCells/AgentiCCells__Website/blob/dev/board/briefs/website-cell-sales-2026-11-17.md)): "the rules of engagement here are 50-50 split. ... if they bring the business, I do the automation, I do the flow, I set that up, and then they can do whatever they want."

- **The split is 50/50 of the sale.** Larger projects are agreed case by case.
- **You bring the business.** You find the customer and own the relationship.
- **We do the automation, the flow and the build.** We set up the team, build the site from the customer's material and hand it over.
- **You get admin access to the delivered site.** The site is static files in a GitHub repository. The customer owns it, and you can be given admin access to it.
- **Our job ends at hand-over.** We sell no subscriptions and give no support.

## What you can sell on your side

At prices you set, and we take no share and no part:

- subscriptions and monthly care
- support and maintenance
- changes and new pages
- installation and deployment to the host the customer wants
- guidance, including making the GitHub repository for the customer

Dinis Cruz's words: "I don't want to sell subscriptions ... I don't want to be providing support. So if somebody else wants to provide support, they can charge." You may use the [price ladder](/cells/) as a starting point or your own prices. The website cell's own price is a first proposal, set in [data/offer.json](https://github.com/AgentiCCells/AgentiCCells__Website/blob/dev/data/offer.json).

This answers the question buyers ask most: who runs the site after hand-over? Your partner, or the customer's own Claude.

## Three kinds of partner

### The freelancer

One person. "For this much, I will make you a website, give you an agentic email and a workflow." You find the customer, collect the photos and the description, send them to us, show the customer the previews and hand over. Then you sell the care.

### The small consultancy

A firm with customers, doing many. Same flow, repeated. Because the setup is automated, a second site is not a second project: the same team, the same policies, a new customer's material. You may keep your own repositories, and customers' work, private.

### The larger firm

Bigger projects, where you want us or Dinis Cruz's network of experts involved: a cell run in the customer's own accounts, several cells, workflows around the site. These are case by case, and the split is agreed before work starts.

## A deal in six steps

1. **Find.** You find a customer who wants a site.
2. **Material.** They give you photos, videos and a description. Some of it can already be public.
3. **Set up.** You send it to us. We set up the cell.
4. **Designs.** The agent team builds, you and the customer see previews and say what to change.
5. **Hand-over.** The repository moves to the customer's GitHub with its full history, and you get admin access.
6. **After.** Our job ends. Yours, if you want it, begins: care, changes, support, at your prices.

## Who owns what

| | Owns it |
|---|---|
| The site, the repository, the customer's material | The customer |
| The customer relationship, and the invoice to the customer | The partner |
| The code and the agent behaviour policies | Open, under the licences on the [open source page](/open-source/) |
| The build and the hand-over | AgentiCCells |

**Payment (proposal):** the partner invoices the customer, and pays AgentiCCells its half soon after the customer pays, within a period to be agreed. This is a proposal, not agreed. It came to us second-hand, so ask us about it.

Any country and any language. The terms are common sense and few. If something here does not fit your case, say so.

## Open source is a way in too

Everything we use is [open](/open-source/). A partner who takes the open source and does it alone is still a win for us, and we will say so. The [build your own website](/build-your-own-website/) page shows the way.

## What we do not do

- We do not compete with you for your customers.
- We name no partner and no customer without their agreement.
- We send nothing on your behalf, and no agent of ours writes to anyone.
