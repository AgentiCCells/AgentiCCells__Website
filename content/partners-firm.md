# For firms: bigger projects, with our orchestration and a network of experts

You run a larger company with bigger projects, and you want us, or Dinis Cruz's network of experts, involved. This page is for you, and you can read it without any other. Nothing has been sold yet, through a partner or otherwise, and no partner has signed. [Email us as a firm](mailto:agent@riskmandate.ai?subject=I%20might%20be%20a%20firm%20partner) when you want to talk.

## What you sell

The website cell is the smallest thing we sell. For bigger projects the team grows: a writer, a reviewer, a data agent, a team for each workflow, with a conductor handing out the work and a person deciding what goes out. Where your customer wants agents to behave under written rules, the behaviour policies come with the cell and can be extended through [riskmandate.ai](https://riskmandate.ai). Dinis Cruz writes of the same pattern run at an event by six people ([the article](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/partners-sell-we-build.html)).

The model is Dinis Cruz's, in his words: "the rules of engagement here are 50-50 split ... if they bring the business, I do the automation, I do the flow, I set that up, and then they can do whatever they want" ([his brief](https://github.com/AgentiCCells/AgentiCCells__Website/blob/dev/board/briefs/website-cell-sales-2026-11-17.md), 2026-11-17). The full programme is in his article, [Partners sell, we build](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/partners-sell-we-build.html) (CC BY 4.0).

## A worked example, from first conversation to hand-over and after

This is an example of how a deal would run, not a record of one: no deal has run.

1. **First conversation.** You and a customer describe a larger project: several cells, a workflow around the site, or a cell run in the customer's own accounts. We talk before any price is set.
2. **Scope.** We agree what we build, who from our network of experts is involved, and the split. Larger projects are agreed case by case, in writing, before work starts.
3. **Set up.** The cell runs in the customer's environment where the work needs it. We keep no customer data.
4. **Build and review.** The agent team does the work, with a person reviewing and deciding what goes out.
5. **Hand-over.** The customer owns the repository and material. You hold the relationship.
6. **After.** You run support and retainers at your prices. We return for new features, templates, workflows and policies, priced on the same 50/50 unless we agree otherwise.

## The money

- **50/50 of the sale** when we are involved. Whoever has the customer invoices them and pays the other half.
- **Payment terms are agreed per deal.**
- **Hosting, support and retainers are yours entirely.** We take no part and no share, and we sell no subscriptions.
- **Prices are yours.** The website cell is {{price}}. The ladder above it, a first proposal, is [£1,500, £5,000 and £20,000](https://github.com/AgentiCCells/AgentiCCells__Website/blob/dev/data/cells.json) (data/cells.json). For a firm the larger steps are case by case.
- Larger projects: the split is agreed case by case before work starts.

## What you need to start

A project in mind, or a customer with one; a person on your side who owns it; a call with us to agree scope and split. Where the customer is regulated, say so early.

## Partner terms

The terms are common sense and few. The customer owns the site, the repository and the material. You own the relationship. Our code and policies are [open](/open-source/), and you may keep your own repositories and your customers' work private. Nobody is named publicly, customer or partner, without agreement. It works in any country and any language, and you invoice in your own currency under your own law. The same terms are on the [partners page](/partners/#partner-terms).
