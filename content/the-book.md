# The book: One Human, Nine Agents, Nineteen Hours

Dinis Cruz wrote a book about how one person and a team of AI agents built a talk in a day, and how to set up your own team and start the same day. Its subtitle is "How a factory of AI agents built a presentation in a day, and how to build yours" (the book's own [Leanpub page](https://leanpub.com/one-human-nine-agents-nineteen-hours)).

The book is a draft. Read it as one. We do not host a copy of the PDF on this site yet; that waits on Dinis.

## Why it is an example of an agentic cell

Dinis argues, in [The cell is the unit](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/the-cell-is-the-unit.html), that a cell is one focused job with an objective, a deliverable and a behaviour policy. The book fits that:

- **Objective.** Write a book from the record of a real day of agent work.
- **Deliverable.** A book, and a public site that shows how it was made.
- **Behaviour policy.** The agents write from the record. The book says that its scenes are dramatised and marked as such, and that its events are the record's (the [Leanpub description](https://leanpub.com/one-human-nine-agents-nineteen-hours)).

It also runs the way we sell: written briefs, a team where each agent has one job written down, a short file that stops everything, and a record anyone can read. The [book site](https://the-ultimate-insider.book.sgit.ai/) keeps that record in the open.

You can buy the same kind of thing from us: a team of agents in your environment that produces a finished piece of work, with every step visible. See [the website cell](/website-cell/) for what that looks like today, and [our own team as a cell](/our-team-as-a-cell/) for the team that keeps this site.
